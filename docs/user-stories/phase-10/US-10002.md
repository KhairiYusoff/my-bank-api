# US-10002 — Dormancy Maintenance Fees

**Phase:** 10
**Repos:** `my-bank-api`
**Status:** 🏗️ In Progress
**Depends on:** US-10001

---

## Value Statement

As the Bank, I want to charge an annual maintenance fee of RM10 on dormant accounts to cover the administrative costs of maintaining these records in our system.

---

## Background

Regulatory guidelines allow banks to charge a small fee for maintaining dormant accounts. This fee is charged annually on the anniversary of the account becoming dormant.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### BE-1: Dormancy Fee Cron
- Daily cron at 02:00 MYT that scans all `dormant` accounts.
- Checks the `statusUpdatedDate` (the date it became dormant).

### BE-2: Fee Rule
- If the current date is exactly 12, 24, 36... months after `statusUpdatedDate`, trigger the fee charge.
- Fee amount: **RM10.00**.

### BE-3: Fee Transaction
- Create a new `Transaction` record:
  - `type`: `"fee"`
  - `amount`: `10.00`
  - `memo`: `"Annual Dormancy Maintenance Fee"`
  - `performedBy`: `null` (indicates system-generated)
  - `status`: `"completed"`
- Debit the `Account.balance` atomically using a Mongoose session.

### BE-4: Balance Handling
- If `balance < 10.00`, charge the remaining balance (reducing it to RM0).
- Log a warning in `ActivityLog` for accounts with zero balance for further review.
