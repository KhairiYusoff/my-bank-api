# US-10005 — Early Account Closure Penalty

**Phase:** 10
**Repos:** `my-bank-api`
**Status:** ⬜ Planned
**Depends on:** US-10004

---

## Value Statement

As the Bank, I want to charge an early closure fee of RM20.00 for accounts closed within 3 months of their opening date, to discourage temporary account use and recover administrative onboarding costs.

---

## Background

Regulatory and business rules permit a penalty for short-term account tenure. This rule applies to Savings, Current, and Business accounts, but specifically excludes Fixed Deposits as they are governed by interest forfeiture rules.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### BE-1: Penalty Logic
- Triggered during the `approveClosure()` process in `account.service.js`.
- Check `dateOpened` against current date.
- If `duration < 3 months` (approx. 90 days), apply the fee.
- **Exception:** Do not apply if `accountType === 'fixed_deposit'`.

### BE-2: Fee Transaction
- Create a new `Transaction` record BEFORE final closure:
  - `type`: `"fee"`
  - `amount`: `20.00`
  - `memo`: `"Early Account Closure Penalty"`
  - `performedBy`: `staffId` (the banker approving the closure)
  - `status`: `"completed"`
- Debit the `Account.balance` (which should have been zeroed out by the customer, but the fee will create a small negative balance that must be settled or aairdropped/waived by staff if they choose — however, standard rule is to deduct from the final withdrawal or require settlement).

### BE-3: Pre-closure Validation Update
- Update `approveClosure` to allow closure even if balance becomes negative due to THIS fee, or ensure the banker can see the fee preview.
- **Revised Rule:** If fee applies, banker must confirm "Charge RM20 early closure fee?" before proceeding.

## Technical Tasks

3. **Frontend:** Update the "Approve Closure" confirmation modal in the Admin Portal to show a warning if an early closure fee will be applied.
