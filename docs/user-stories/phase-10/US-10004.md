# US-10004 — Managed Account Closure

**Phase:** 10

**Status:** 🏗️ In Progress
**Depends on:** Phase 9 complete ✅

---

## Value Statement

As a Customer, I want to request the closure of my account, and as a Banker, I want to review and approve these requests once the balance is zero, ensuring a clean exit from our services.

---

## Background

Account closure is a formal process. Customers cannot self-close instantly; they must request it, and the bank must verify that there are no pending obligations and the balance is zero before final closure. Benchmarking Malaysian banks, this is a banking operation performed by branch staff (Bankers), not IT/Systems staff (Admins).

---

## Acceptance Criteria

### BE-1: Closure Request
- **Endpoint:** `POST /api/accounts/:accountNumber/close-request`
- **Auth:** `customer` (owner only).
- Sets `Account.status` to `pending_closure`.
- Log `ACCOUNT_CLOSE_REQUESTED` in `ActivityLog`.

### BE-2: Closure Approval
- **Endpoint:** `POST /api/accounts/:accountNumber/approve-closure`
- **Auth:** `banker` role only.
- **Constraint:** Reject if `Account.balance > 0`. Error: "Account balance must be zero before closure."
- Sets `Account.status` to `closed`.
- Sets `dateClosed` to current timestamp.
- Log `ACCOUNT_CLOSED` in `ActivityLog`.

### BE-3: Data Preservation & Visibility
- `closed` accounts are **not** deleted from the database (for auditing and regulatory compliance).
- They must be filtered out from:
  - Customer Dashboard list.
  - Transfer/Withdraw/Deposit account selectors.
  - Expense payment method selectors.

### FE-1: Customer Request UI
- Add a "Close Account" section in `AccountDetailsPage` (perhaps under a "Settings" or "Actions" tab).
- Provide clear instructions that balance must be RM0 before request.

### FE-2: Banker Approval UI
- Add "Approve Closure" button in Admin Portal's `AccountDetailPage` visible only when status is `pending_closure`.
