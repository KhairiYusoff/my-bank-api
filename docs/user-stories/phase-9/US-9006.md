## Value Statement

As a **Customer**, I want to manage my Fixed Deposit maturity instructions (Auto-Renew toggle) and receive timely alerts, so that I can decide in advance whether to renew my investment or have it settled automatically.

---

## Background

Business Rule 1.3 states that FDs auto-renew if no action is taken within the grace period. Providing a toggle for `autoRenew` allows customers to "pre-set" their preference. 

- If `autoRenew` is **ON**: On Day 8, the principal rolls over for a new term.
- If `autoRenew` is **OFF**: On Day 8, the principal is automatically paid out and the FD is closed.

This gives customers control over their funds without requiring manual intervention during the grace period if they've already made their decision.

## Acceptance Criteria

### BE-1: Update Maturity Instructions (`PATCH /api/accounts/:accountNumber/fd-instructions`)

**Auth:** Customer (Owner only).

1.  Update `autoRenew` (boolean) on the Account document.
2.  Update `linkedAccount` (string) if the customer wants to change the destination for interest/principal.
3.  Log `UPDATE_FD_INSTRUCTIONS` in `ActivityLog`.

### BE-2: Pre-Maturity Notification

-   Cron job (or added to US-9003 engine) checks for FDs maturing in exactly 7 days.
-   Send notification: "Your FD {number} will mature on {date}. Current instruction: {Auto-Renew ON/OFF}."

### FE-1: FD Settings UI

-   In `AccountDetailsPage` for FD accounts, add a "Settings" or "Maturity Instructions" section.
-   Toggle switch for "Auto-Renew Principal".
-   Dropdown to select/change "Linked Account" for settlement.
