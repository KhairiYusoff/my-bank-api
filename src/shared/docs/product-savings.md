# MyBank Savings Account

## Overview
The MyBank Savings Account is a flexible deposit account designed to help customers grow their savings with competitive interest rates and easy access to funds.

## Key Features
- Minimum opening balance: RM 500
- No monthly fees if balance is maintained above RM 500
- Monthly fee of RM 5 if balance falls below RM 500
- Unlimited free ATM withdrawals at MyBank ATMs
- Online and mobile banking access included

## Interest Rates
- Balance RM 0 – RM 10,000: 1.85% per annum
- Balance RM 10,001 – RM 50,000: 2.10% per annum
- Balance above RM 50,000: 2.40% per annum
- Interest is calculated daily and credited monthly

## Eligibility
- Malaysian citizens and permanent residents aged 18 and above
- Valid MyKad or passport required
- Can be opened jointly (up to 2 account holders)

## Withdrawals & Transfers
- Instant transfers via DuitNow or IBG
- Cash withdrawals via ATM or over-the-counter
- No limit on number of withdrawals per month

## How to Open
- Apply online via MyBank app or website
- Walk-in to any MyBank branch
- Required documents: MyKad / passport, proof of address

## Account Closure
- No penalty for closing the account
- Remaining balance will be refunded via cheque or transfer
