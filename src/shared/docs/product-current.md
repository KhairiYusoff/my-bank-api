# MyBank Current Account

## Overview
The MyBank Current Account is an everyday transactional account for individuals and businesses who need frequent access to funds without restrictions.

## Key Features
- Minimum opening balance: RM 1,000
- No interest earned (purely transactional account)
- Unlimited transactions per month
- Comes with a cheque book facility
- Overdraft facility available upon approval

## Fees
- Monthly maintenance fee: RM 10 (waived if monthly average balance ≥ RM 5,000)
- Cheque book issuance: RM 10 per book (25 leaves)
- Dishonoured cheque fee: RM 50 per incident

## Overdraft Facility
- Approved overdraft limit based on creditworthiness
- Interest charged at base rate + 1.5% per annum on amount utilised
- Auto-repaid when funds are deposited

## Transactions
- Real-time DuitNow transfers (instant)
- IBG transfers (same day or next business day)
- SWIFT for international transfers
- Cheque payments

## Eligibility
- Malaysian citizens aged 18 and above, or registered businesses
- Valid MyKad / business registration documents
- Minimum opening deposit of RM 1,000

## How to Open
- Visit any MyBank branch with required documents
- Online application available for individuals
- Business accounts require additional company documents (Form 9, Form 49, board resolution)

## Account Closure
- No penalty for closing
- Outstanding cheques must be cleared before closure
- Overdraft balance must be fully settled
