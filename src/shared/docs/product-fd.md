# MyBank Fixed Deposit (FD) Account

## Overview
The MyBank Fixed Deposit Account offers guaranteed high returns by locking in a lump sum for a fixed tenure. Ideal for customers with surplus funds they do not need in the short term.

## Key Features
- Guaranteed fixed interest rate for the full tenure
- Higher returns compared to savings accounts
- Available in tenures from 1 month to 60 months
- Auto-renewal option available
- Islamic FD option (Mudarabah-based) also available

## Interest Rates (indicative, subject to change)
| Tenure        | Annual Interest Rate |
|---------------|----------------------|
| 1 month       | 2.80%               |
| 3 months      | 3.00%               |
| 6 months      | 3.20%               |
| 12 months     | 3.50%               |
| 24 months     | 3.65%               |
| 36 months     | 3.75%               |
| 60 months     | 3.90%               |

## Minimum Placement
- Minimum: RM 1,000
- No maximum limit

## Early Withdrawal
- Allowed but incurs a penalty
- Interest forfeited for the remaining tenure
- Principal is returned in full

## At Maturity
- Auto-renewed by default (principal + interest rolled over)
- Customer can choose to change tenure or withdraw at maturity
- Maturity notification sent via SMS and email

## Eligibility
- Malaysian citizens aged 18 and above
- Existing MyBank savings or current account required
- Joint FD allowed (up to 2 holders)

## How to Open
- Apply via MyBank online banking or mobile app
- Walk-in to any MyBank branch
- Required: MyKad / passport, existing MyBank account

## Tax
- Interest income may be subject to withholding tax for non-residents
- Malaysian residents: interest income is tax-exempt up to RM 2,000 per year
