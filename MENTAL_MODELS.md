# Mental Models for System Design & Product Thinking

A collection of principles to guide decisions before writing a single line of code.

---

## Notifications

> "Notify when value moves, or when the ability to protect value is at risk. Everything else is noise."

**Value moves** → money transferred, bill paid, crypto sold  
**Ability to protect value is at risk** → new device login, password changed, 2FA disabled  
**Everything else** → todo ticked, expense logged, profile updated = silence
